Infinite Fracture Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Infinite Fracture is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Alex Eachus ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

In short: the game shares gameplay data only if you agree to it, and sends a bug report only when you choose to send one. Both are linked to you through a random player ID the game creates on your device, never through your name, email address or Apple ID. Privacy laws treat that random ID as personal data, and so does this policy. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

The random ID

The first time the game sends anything to its server (after you agree to share gameplay data, or when you send a bug report), it creates a random player ID on your device. Everything the game sends is tied to this ID, so the data held on the server is linked to you through it. The ID is not your name, email address, Apple ID, device identifier or advertising identifier, and the game never connects it to any of them.

What is collected

Gameplay analytics, only after you opt in

After your first run, the game asks once whether it can send me gameplay data so I can see what needs balancing. Nothing is collected before you answer, and nothing at all unless you choose Sure. If you do, the game records how it is played and sends it to me: for example how long you play, the runs you play and their scores, where in a level runs end and hearts are lost, what you buy in the in-run shop, shards and dust earned and spent, quests completed, geodes opened, items unlocked, and store items shown and bought. Each record carries the random ID and the game's version.

Bug reports, only when you send one

Settings → More → Report a bug lets you send me a description of a problem. A report is sent only when you press Send, even if analytics is off, because you chose to send it. It contains whatever you write, your device model (for example "iPhone15,2"), its operating system version, the game's version, a summary of your last run (the realm, the score and how it ended) and the random ID. The screen lists exactly what is attached before you send. Please do not put personal details such as your name, email address or phone number in the description.

The CURE Childhood Cancer screen

When you launch the game, it downloads a short public message from my server for the CURE Childhood Cancer screen. That request doesn't include your player ID or any gameplay data, and it is sent whether or not you have opted in to sharing gameplay data.

IP addresses

Whenever the game connects to its server, the server provider, Supabase, receives your device's IP address, as with any internet connection. The game does not record it in the gameplay data or bug reports; Supabase handles it as described in its privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (scores, unlocks, currencies, statistics) and your settings are saved on your device and are never sent to me. They may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its terms. Reset save in the game's settings erases your progress on the device.

Why

I use this information only to understand how the game is played, to improve it, and to find and fix problems. There is no advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The data is never sold, never shared with anyone else, and never combined with other data to identify you.

The only service that processes it is Supabase, which stores it on my behalf on servers in the United States.

Your choices

  • The consent prompt. After your first run the game asks once; gameplay data is only shared if you choose Sure.
  • The Analytics switch. Settings → More → Privacy → Analytics turns sharing on or off at any time. Turning it off stops sending immediately and deletes anything recorded on your device that had not been sent yet.
  • Delete my data. Settings → More → Privacy → Delete my data deletes everything the server holds for your random ID (gameplay data, bug reports and the ID itself), and the game starts a fresh random ID. Your game progress on the device is not affected.
  • Email. You can email alexeachus@me.com with your random ID, which is shown in Settings → More → Report a bug with a Copy button, to ask about, receive or delete your data.

How long it is kept

Gameplay data and bug reports are kept for 12 months from when they are received, then deleted automatically. A random ID that has not been used for 180 days is deleted too.

Children

Infinite Fracture shows no ads and builds no profiles of anyone, including children. Any data it collects is used only to run and improve the game.

Players in the EU and UK

Gameplay data is processed on the basis of your consent, given through the consent prompt or the Analytics switch, and a bug report on the basis of your choice to send it. You have the right to access the data held for your random ID, to have it deleted, and to withdraw your consent at any time (turning Analytics off does not affect data processed before you did). To exercise these rights, use the in-game options above or email alexeachus@me.com with your random ID. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

In-app purchases

Purchases in Infinite Fracture are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it for you. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If Infinite Fracture begins to handle information differently, this page will be updated, and its "last updated" date changed, before that version of the game is released.
