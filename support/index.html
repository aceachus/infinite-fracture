Support

Help with Infinite Fracture.

Report a bug in the game

Open Settings → More → Report a bug, describe what happened, and press Send. The screen lists exactly what is attached to your description: your random player ID, the game's version, your device model and operating system version, and a summary of your last run. Please don't include personal details such as your name or email address in the description. A report is sent even if Analytics is off, because you chose to send it. You can send up to three reports a day.

Contact

Email alexeachus@me.com. If your question is about a bug or about your data, include your random player ID: it is shown in Settings → More → Report a bug, with a Copy button. You can also share it on Discord the same way.

Delete your data

Open Settings → More → Privacy and press Delete my data. It deletes everything the server holds for your random ID (gameplay data, bug reports and the ID itself), and the game starts a new random ID the next time it connects. Your game progress lives on your device and is not affected; Reset save is the separate button for that.

You can also email alexeachus@me.com with your random player ID and ask for your data to be deleted or sent to you.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
