Infinite Fracture Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Infinite Fracture is a game for iPhone made by Alex Eachus. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

In short: Infinite Fracture does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Infinite Fracture saves your game progress (scores, unlocks, currencies and gameplay statistics) and your settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. You can erase it at any time with Reset Save in the game's settings, or by deleting the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases in Infinite Fracture are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it for you. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

No third-party services

Infinite Fracture contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Infinite Fracture does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Infinite Fracture ever begins to handle information differently, this page will be updated and its effective date changed before that version of the game is released.
