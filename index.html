Infinite Fracture Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Infinite Fracture is a game for iPhone made by Alex Eachus. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

In short: Infinite Fracture collects anonymous gameplay usage data to help improve the game. It is not linked to your identity, contains no personal information, and you can turn it off at any time in the game's settings. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Infinite Fracture saves your game progress (scores, unlocks, currencies and gameplay statistics) and your settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Your save itself is never sent to me or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. You can erase it at any time with Reset Save in the game's settings, or by deleting the app.

Anonymous gameplay usage data

While Analytics is on (it is on by default), the game records what happens in play and sends it to me: for example the runs you play and their scores, shards and dust earned and spent, quests completed, geodes opened, items unlocked, and store items shown and bought. Each record carries the game's version and a random identifier the game creates the first time it runs. That identifier is anonymous: it is not your name, email address, Apple ID, device identifier or advertising identifier, and it is not linked to any of them.

The game never collects your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the data is never sold, used for advertising, or combined with other data to identify you. I use it only to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

The data is stored with Supabase, which hosts it on my behalf on servers in the United States. As with any internet connection, the servers that receive it can see your device's IP address; the game does not record it.

Turning it off: open Settings → Data → Analytics in the game and switch it off. The game stops sending immediately and deletes anything recorded on your device that had not been sent yet.

In-app purchases

Purchases in Infinite Fracture are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it for you. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

No advertising or tracking

Infinite Fracture contains no advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Its only outside service is Supabase, which stores the anonymous usage data described above.

Children

Infinite Fracture does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous usage data contains no personal information.

Changes to this policy

If Infinite Fracture ever begins to handle information differently, this page will be updated and its effective date changed before that version of the game is released.
