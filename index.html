Infinite Fracture

A roguelike platformer for iPhone by Alex Eachus.

Race across crumbling floating realms as they break apart behind you. Dash, wall-jump and ride cables through hand-built chunks of terrain, pick sigils that change how you play, and see how far you get before the collapse catches you.
